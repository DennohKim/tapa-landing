Questions organisers ask

The short version of how Tapa behaves when a real night gets messy.

What if the venue has no signal?

Terminals keep selling. Each one can take sales on trust up to an offline allowance you set, queues them, and sends them as soon as it reconnects. A sale can never be charged twice.

What happens when a guest loses their band?

The help desk freezes it from the console straight away and links a replacement. The balance lives in the guest's wallet, not on the band, so nothing is lost.

Can a cashier undo a mistake?

Yes, on the terminal, within the reversal window. After that the help desk reverses it from the console, and the audit log records who did it and why.

How and when do stands get paid?

During the night, not days after. Each stand sees its sales come in as they happen, and the money reaches its own wallet while the event is still going. Fees come off along the way and at close.

What happens to money a guest does not spend?

It stays in their own wallet. There is no organiser float and no refund queue at the end of the night.

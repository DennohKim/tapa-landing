Picked the name for Expendi's NFC event wristband. Shortlist was Expendi Tap, TapBand, Bango, Cuff and Tapa.

Went with **Tapa**: sounds like "tap", short enough to print on a band, and reads naturally in "tap your Tapa at the bar."

Set up the project folder with two parts: the vendor POS app and this landing site with a devlog.
